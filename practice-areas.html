Matters Available for Mediation

Each mediation is approached with impartiality, preparation and an understanding of the risks faced by both plaintiffs and defendants.

01

Personal Injury

Claims involving bodily injury, disputed liability, medical expenses, lost income, future care and other alleged damages.

02

Trucking and Commercial Vehicle

Collisions involving commercial vehicles, trucking companies, professional drivers, contractors, insurance carriers and potentially complex damages.

03

Wrongful Death

Sensitive and consequential claims arising from a fatal incident, with careful attention to liability, damages and the needs of all parties.

04

Premises Liability

Claims involving alleged unsafe property conditions, slip-and-fall incidents, negligent security and other property-related injuries.

05

Insurance Disputes

Disputes involving claim valuation, insurance coverage and the respective positions of claimants, insureds and insurance carriers.

06

Construction Defect

Disputes involving alleged construction defects, property damage, responsibility among multiple parties and related insurance issues.

The Mediation Process

Focused on Meaningful Evaluation and Resolution

Hugh’s experience representing both plaintiffs and defendants allows him to understand the competing risks presented by civil litigation.

Practical Case Evaluation

The parties are encouraged to carefully evaluate the strengths, weaknesses and uncertainty of continued litigation.

Balanced Discussion

Each side receives a fair opportunity to explain its position while confidential communications remain protected.

Continued Effort

When a matter does not resolve during the initial session, Hugh remains willing to help the parties continue working toward settlement.
