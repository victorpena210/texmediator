Scheduling Information

Cancellation Policy

Please review the cancellation and postponement requirements before reserving a mediation session.

Written Notice Required

All cancellations and postponements must be submitted in writing.

The applicable cancellation terms depend on when the written notice is received.

Cancellation Deadlines

Policy Based on Notice Timing

Refund or Credit

More Than 10 Business Days

Cancellations received more than 10 business days before the mediation will receive a full refund or may be applied as a credit to a rescheduled date.

Cancellation Fee

Within 10 Business Days

Cancellations received within 10 business days of the mediation are subject to a 50% cancellation fee.

Non-Refundable

Within 48 Business Hours

Cancellations received within 48 business hours of the mediation are non-refundable and the full mediation fee is due, unless the mediator elects to apply the payment as a credit toward a rescheduled mediation.
