Website Information

Privacy Policy

This policy explains how information submitted through this website and its scheduling services is handled.

Last Updated August 12, 2026

Protecting Your Information

Hugh Howerton and TexMediator P.L.L.C. use information submitted through this website only for scheduling, administering and conducting mediation services.

Information

Information Collected

Information may include your name, law firm, email address, phone number, party represented, case-identification information, scheduling selections, notes and requested documents.

Use

How Information Is Used

Information is used to review mediation requests, communicate with participants, check scheduling and conflicts, arrange Zoom sessions and administer accepted mediations.

Service Providers

Third-Party Services

Scheduling information is processed through Acuity Scheduling. Confirmed mediations may use Zoom. These companies process information under their own privacy policies.

Important Warning

Confidential Materials

Do not submit confidential settlement positions, privileged communications, medical records or other sensitive case materials through the website. Submit only the requested scheduling, case-identification and original-petition information.

Protection

Security and Retention

Reasonable administrative and technical measures are used to protect submitted information. Information is retained only as reasonably necessary for mediation administration, legal obligations and recordkeeping.

Contact

Questions or Requests

Questions concerning personal information may be directed to hugh@texmediator.com or (713) 822-8904.
