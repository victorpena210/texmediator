About Hugh

A Trial-Tested Perspective on Mediation

Hugh understands the uncertainty, expense and pressure that attorneys and their clients face when a case approaches trial.

During more than 30 years of personal-injury trial practice, Hugh represented both plaintiffs and defendants and tried more than 200 cases to verdict. That experience gives him firsthand insight into how each side evaluates liability, damages, evidence and the risks of proceeding to trial.

Hugh believes effective mediation requires more than carrying demands and offers between rooms. He works to identify the issues driving the dispute, discuss the strengths and weaknesses of the case fairly and help everyone evaluate realistic paths toward resolution.

His goal is to help the parties reach an amount and resolution they can accept. When a matter does not settle during the scheduled session, Hugh remains willing to stay involved and continue working with the parties when further discussion may be productive.

Neutrality and confidentiality guide every mediation. Information shared privately with Hugh remains confidential unless he receives express permission to disclose it.

Professional Credentials

Hugh is Board Certified in Personal Injury Trial Law by the Texas Board of Legal Specialization and is AV rated by Martindale-Hubbell.
