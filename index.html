Mediation Approach

My job is to get your case settled

Having worked on behalf of both plaintiffs and defendants, I understand the risks each side faces at trial. Trial can be expensive, stressful and unpredictable.

My role is to help both sides reach a resolution that is smarter, faster, and more certain than continuing litigation. I remain completely neutral, but I am actively engaged in guiding the discussion so each of you can clearly evaluate the strengths, weaknesses, and risks of your positions.

This process gives you something the courtroom cannot: control. Instead of leaving the outcome to a judge or jury, mediation allows you to shape an agreement that protects your interests, manages risk, and brings closure. I will help you focus on what matters most—liability, damages, and the true cost of uncertainty—so you can make informed decisions and move forward with confidence.

Information shared with me privately will remain confidential unless I receive express permission to disclose it.
