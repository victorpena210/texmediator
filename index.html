Mediation Approach

A Mediator Who Works Toward Resolution

My job is to help get your case settled at an amount everyone can accept.

Having worked on behalf of both plaintiffs and defendants, I understand the risks each side faces at trial. Trial can be expensive, stressful and unpredictable.

A good mediator does more than carry demands and offers between rooms. I intend to discuss the important issues in the case, including its strengths and weaknesses, fairly and without favor to either side.

If the mediation results in an impasse, I will remain involved in the hope of helping the parties resolve the case at a later time.

Information shared with me privately will remain confidential unless I receive express permission to disclose it.
